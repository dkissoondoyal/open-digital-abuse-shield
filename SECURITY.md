# Security policy

ODAS v0.1 is research software, not an email protection product. Do not deploy it as an enforcement gateway. It does not perform cryptographic verification or DNS queries.

Never upload real private emails, credentials, tokens, or sensitive headers to public issues. Use synthetic fixtures or redact personal data. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to repository maintainers once a security contact is published.

Threat intelligence submissions must not be treated as authoritative until independently verified. Future feed updates must be authenticated, versioned, expiring, reversible and auditable.
